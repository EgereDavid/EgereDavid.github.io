About

The person behind the work.

Engineer by training, entrepreneur by choice. Here's the full picture.

Hi, I'm Ugochukwu.

I'm a Technical Support Engineer and Cybersecurity professional based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. My work sits at the intersection of two things I care about: making technology actually work for the person using it, and making sure it's safe while doing so.

I'm currently pursuing a BSc in Systems Administration at Ensign College, and I hold a Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate alongside networking and technical support credentials. I've built that foundation through hands-on troubleshooting: hardware diagnostics, network issues, ticketing systems, and end-user training, not just theory.

Outside of client work, I founded and run SecureWithDavid Tech Hub, where I bring the same engineering discipline to individuals and small businesses who need reliable tech support, device sales, and networking help.

How I Work

Principles I don't compromise on.

Diagnose before you fix

Every issue gets root-caused first. Patch-and-hope isn't a strategy I use.

Security is not optional

Every setup I touch gets evaluated with a security-first mindset, not as an afterthought.

Document everything

If I fixed it, I wrote it down, for the next person and for myself.

Explain, don't just execute

End users deserve to understand what happened, not just that it's "fixed now."
